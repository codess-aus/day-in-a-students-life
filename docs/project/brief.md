# Assignment brief: student survival hub

**All names, places, dates, source notes, and costs in this project pack are
fictional teaching material. They are not real campus advice or research evidence.**

## The situation

New students at Example University struggle to find useful study information.
Your group will publish a small, accessible hub that helps a student decide
what to do next without hunting through multiple chats.

The deadline is **Friday at 8 pm**, not Thursday. This brief is authoritative
when the meeting notes disagree.

## Required outcome

- A MkDocs site published with GitHub Pages.
- A short campus-resource guide that clearly labels fictional information.
- A browser-side JavaScript study planner that allocates a chosen number of
  whole study minutes across unfinished tasks.
- Clear empty and invalid-input states, labelled controls, and keyboard access.
- A reviewed contribution and evidence of checks.

The starter divides time equally across a task count. Your coding contribution
introduces named tasks and completion status. Minutes may differ by one to avoid
losing time to rounding. All-completed input needs an explicit message, not an
invalid calculation.

## Constraints

- Use Markdown, MkDocs configuration, CSS, and plain JavaScript. No backend.
- No accounts, real student data, tracking, paid APIs, or new framework.
- No requirement to use a particular Copilot model or paid feature.
- Keep the working starter usable while improving it.
- Put decisions and review evidence in issues and pull requests.
