# Campus resources

**Fictional workshop content. These are not real campus locations or services.**

## Have a short gap?

Choose a concrete task and a checkpoint. For example, try an exercise, explain
your answer without looking, then compare it with your notes.
This advice is based on [supplied notes S1 and S2](../project/readings.md#s1-recognising-versus-retrieving),
not a claim that one method guarantees success.
