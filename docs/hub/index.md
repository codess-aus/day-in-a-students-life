# Student survival hub

**A fictional Example University project for this workshop. Not real campus advice.**

You have a short gap between classes. Choose one useful next action:

- [Plan a study block](planner.md): divide your available time into small tasks.
- [Find a campus resource](resources.md): explore the starter guide.

This is the student-facing part of the repository. Participants improve these
pages during [Alex's day](../workshop/index.md).

The starter planner works without accounts or a backend. It does not store your
inputs. Use example tasks rather than personal or sensitive information.
